Abertura da aula · antes do material

Lead-in: o que ela já sabe sobre influência

Três perguntas orais antes de abrir qualquer tela. O objetivo é ativar experiência real e criar o contexto emocional para o conceito de filtros. Não corrija nada aqui — só ouça e note.

Q01 Think of a time you needed approval from someone senior for something you believed in. What happened?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
Does she describe what she said — or does she describe who the person was and how they responded? The first signals she is still thinking from her own perspective. The second signals she is already thinking about the filter.
Observe
If she says "I prepared really well but it still did not work" — that is the perfect entry point for the filter concept.
Connects to: The Filter — the moment the answer shifts from "I said X" to "they cared about Y" is when the concept lands.
Q02 Have you ever presented the same idea to two different people and gotten completely different reactions?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
Listen for her to describe the difference between the two people — not the difference in how she presented. If she says "I changed my approach" she is close. If she says "they were just different people," that is the moment to ask: "What was different about them?"
Connects to: The five filters — use her example as the first data point. "So what you are describing is that one of them had a financial filter and the other had a strategic filter."
Transition
Good. What you are describing is not just different personalities — it is different decision filters. Every stakeholder has one. And once you know the filter, the same information becomes a completely different conversation. Let me show you what I mean.
Q03 Before we look at the filters — which of these five roles do you find hardest to communicate with? CEO, CFO, COO, Director, or CHRO?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
Her answer will tell you which filter she finds hardest to read. CFO = she struggles with financial logic. CEO = she struggles with strategic framing. Director = she struggles with political navigation. Use this to personalise the session.
Observe
Watch for hesitation. If she cannot pick one, she may be avoiding the hardest one. Gently push: "Which one makes you most nervous before the meeting?"
Connects to: The filter card for whichever role she names — go there first.
The Filter · Seção 01 do módulo

Identificar o filtro em tempo real

Depois que ela leu os filter cards, essas perguntas testam se o conceito saiu do abstrato para o aplicado. A pergunta sobre Adriano é o momento mais importante desta seção.

Q04 Look at Adriano's opening line. Without thinking — what is your gut reaction? What do you want to say back?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
Most people's gut reaction is defensive: "I had the right to talk to your team." That is the wrong move with a political filter. If she goes defensive, hold that — do not correct immediately. Ask: "And if you said that — what do you think Adriano does next?"
Observe
If she says something that acknowledges his concern first, name it: "Notice what you just did — you acknowledged his process before you made your case. That is what opens a political filter."
Connects to: Political filter — the gap between gut reaction and effective response is exactly where the political filter concept lands.
Q05 Now look at Carlos. What does his opening tell you about how he makes decisions?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
She should pick up on the three-question structure: what is happening / what have you done / what do you need. If she does not notice the pattern, point to it: "Count the questions. What does that tell you about what he cares about?"
Connects to: Operational filter — "who owns this, what is the plan" as the recurring phrase.
Transition
Now she has read two filters in real time. This is the skill: seeing the filter in the first sentence, not after 20 minutes of conversation.
Q06 Which of the five recurring phrases do you find hardest to respond to in English? "Show me the numbers" / "What is the plan" / "How does this look" / "What is the bigger picture" / "Why are you coming to me with this?"
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
The answer reveals which filter she finds hardest to translate for — and which vocabulary needs the most work. Spend extra time on whichever phrase she picks. Build the response together before moving on.
Connects to: Language practice — the recurring phrase becomes the entry point for the vocabulary work.
The Timing · Seção 02 do módulo

Ler a janela de decisão

Conecta o conceito de timing a uma experiência real dela — e depois aplica ao cenário de Valore Bank. A pergunta Q08 é a prática principal desta seção.

Q07 Think about a proposal you made that was rejected. Looking back — was the window actually open?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
Listen for context clues: budget freeze, political conflict, a decision that had just been made on the same topic. If she says "they just were not interested" — ask: "What was happening in the organisation at that moment?" Help her see the context she may have missed.
Observe
If the window was closed and she did not realise it, that is a powerful moment. "So the rejection was not about the idea — it was about the timing."
Connects to: Window open/closed — use her example to anchor the concept before showing the framework.
Q08 It is Thursday afternoon at Valore Bank. What do you do in the next 4 hours — and why?
What to watch
This is the main practice question. Let her answer fully before showing the reference. Listen for: (1) Does she mention Carlos? (2) Does she mention urgency / why Thursday not Friday? (3) Does she think about Adriano's weekend preparation?
Observe
If she skips Carlos: "Who else knows about this situation right now? Who should know before you face Adriano?"
Connects to: The timing practice in the module — use the reference only after she has committed to an answer.
Transition
Good. She now has the window concept and has applied it to Valore Bank. Move to the sequence.
The Sequence · Seção 03 do módulo

Quem primeiro — e por quê

O foco aqui é a aplicação real: quem é o Carlos dela na empresa dela. O desafio final (Q11) integra todos os três conceitos e deve ser usado como avaliação informal do que ficou.

Q09 In your company — who is your Carlos? Is there someone with that COO profile — operational, pragmatic, structural access to the CEO?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
She may name someone immediately or hesitate. If she hesitates: "Think about who you would go to if you needed something to reach the CEO without going directly." That person is her Carlos. If she says she does not have one — that is a strategic gap worth naming.
Connects to: Internal sponsor concept — her real Carlos is the most powerful anchor for the concept.
Q10 Activate Carlos: write your opening and your specific ask.
What to watch
The most common error: the ask is too vague ("support me"). The goal is a specific, time-bound ask: "hold the escalation for 48 hours." If her ask is vague, ask her: "If Carlos said yes to that — what exactly would he do differently tomorrow morning?" If she cannot answer, the ask is too vague.
Observe
Watch for whether she acknowledges what she did (spoke to the team without telling Adriano) before making the ask. If she does not, role-play Carlos pushing back: "But why did you not come to me first?"
Connects to: The sequence practice in the module.
Q11 Final challenge: Filter + Timing + Sequence all at once. It is Monday. The CEO forwarded Adriano's email. "Can we talk?" What do you do?
What to watch
This is the hardest question. She needs to: (1) identify the CEO's filter (strategic) (2) read the window (open but narrow) (3) brief Carlos before calling back. Time her: she has 10 minutes to prepare in the scenario. Does she use them well?
Observe
After she answers, ask: "What was the first thing you thought about when you read that message?" That tells you where she starts instinctively — and whether the module shifted it.
Connects to: The final practice in the module.
Encerramento da aula

Conectar de volta à realidade — e medir o que ficou

Três perguntas que fecham o aprendizado. A Q14 é a mais importante: especificidade na resposta sinaliza que o conceito saiu do módulo e entrou na prática.

Q12 Of the five filters — which one do you find hardest to adapt to in English? Why?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
The language difficulty and the conceptual difficulty are often the same filter. If she says "political filter" — she probably struggles with diplomatic language. If she says "financial filter" — she probably struggles with numbers language. Use this to assign vocabulary practice for the next session.
Q13 Think about a stakeholder you need to influence in the next two weeks. Which filter do they use? Is the window open?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
This is the bridge to real life. She should be able to name a filter and read a window for a real person in her company. If she can do this fluently, the module has landed. If she struggles, it signals the concept needs more practice with real examples.
Connects to: The reflection textarea in the lead-in — compare her answer now to what she wrote at the start. Has the language changed?
Q14 What is one thing you will do differently in your next stakeholder conversation because of today?
◉ Oral — não precisa escrever
What to listen for
Listen for specificity. "I will think about the filter first" is still abstract. "Before I talk to Rafael next Tuesday, I will ask myself what his first question will be — and answer that first" is concrete. Push for the concrete version.
